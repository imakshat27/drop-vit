# Drop — Instagram introduction kit

Upload `drop-instagram-logo-1080x1080.png` as the profile image. Its horizontal logo sits safely inside Instagram’s circular crop.

Post images are 1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5). Publish separately in numbered order, or as two slides in one introduction carousel. These are launch introduction assets, not paid ads. No student data or fabricated usage counts are included.

## Post 01 — Meet Drop

Meet Drop: a curated space for what VIT students are building.

Explore projects, meet the people behind them, and submit something you’ve made. Your side project deserves to leave the group chat.

Browse at droppp.tech. Sign in with your VIT Google account to join.

#Drop #VIT #StudentBuilders #BuildInPublic

## Post 02 — The Weekly Drop

Built here. Dropped weekly.

The Weekly Drop is our weekly exhibition of curated student builds. Submit your project for review, explore the lineup, and support the work you love.

Each verified VIT account can support each project once per weekly edition. Weekly results join the archive, and achievements live on in Honors.

See the lineup at droppp.tech/weekly.

#Drop #WeeklyDrop #VIT #StudentProjects

## Design and exports

Black #000000, white #FFFFFF, neon lime #DBFF76, lilac #C6B0FF. Arial bold and Courier New; the same solid eight-spoke SVG mark used by the website, with no text-glyph substitute and no hollow centre.

Editable HTML sources are included. `node assets/social/introducing-drop/render.mjs` regenerates PNGs with Playwright and reads the canonical mark from `src/lib/brand-mark.ts`. The SVG logo uses editable Arial text; the PNG is the exact upload-ready asset.
